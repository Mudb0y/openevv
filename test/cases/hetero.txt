# What the heteronym filter should and should not write.
#
# One case a line. The words the engine already turns correctly are here too,
# because the filter must leave those alone: writing an annotation where the
# engine was right is as wrong as missing one where it was not.
I bought some produce at the store.
I produce music.
the produce was fresh.
they will produce it.
the transport is late.
they will transport it.
we must transport it.
the export is large.
we must export it.
the transfer is done.
they will transfer it.
the digest is short.
they must digest it.
the decrease is small.
the transplant is ready.
they will transplant it.
the record is here.
please record this.
I read the book.
I will read it.
the wind is cold.
they will wind it.
nothing here changes at all.
