The engine was written to be driven a sentence at a time, and every case in the other files is one sentence long. This one is not. It runs to several sentences in a row, so that the queue between the text side and the synthesis thread is asked to hold more than one buffer's worth, and so that whatever hands samples back has to do it many times rather than once. If anything in the platform layer loses a wakeup, this is where it shows.
A shorter run of three sentences. Each one ends with a full stop, so the engine has somewhere to put a pause. Between them the synthesis thread goes idle and is woken again, which is the part that a single sentence never exercises.
Numbers and punctuation across a long stretch: 1, 2, 3, then 47 and 1,234 and 5.67 per cent. Dr. Smith, Mr. Jones and Mrs. Brown-Taylor arrived at 7:45 p.m. on the 3rd of May. They said, don't worry; it is only a test. The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog, and then does it again, and again, until somebody stops it.
