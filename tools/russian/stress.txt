# Where the stress falls in the Russian words heard most, for
# src/eci/synth/eci_russian.c. One word to a line, lowercase except the
# stressed vowel, which is a capital. ё is always stressed and needs no
# capital. A word of one syllable needs no line, and neither does a word the
# rules in eci_russian.c already place right, and neither does a word
# OpenRussian's table (openrussian.tsv.gz) already has right: what is here is
# what it lacks -- pronouns, particles, names -- and where it gives a rarer
# reading than the common one: кому́ rather than ко́му, сто́ит rather than
# стои́т. This wins over that table.
#
# tools/russian/stress.py turns this into eci_russian_stress.h.
